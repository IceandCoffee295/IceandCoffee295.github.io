About

Hi, I'm
그림Caffeine.

Hi, I'm Geurim Caffeine, an illustrator and designer. For me, everything starts with drawing. Whether it's a character sheet for a streamer, a window graphic for a restaurant, or a quick sketch in class, I just love drawing things.

I don't stick to just one style. I love drawing anime characters just as much as realistic paintings, and moving between the two keeps my work fresh and pushes my skills in both directions. I focus on character design and digital illustration, especially for VTubers and streamers, and I'm currently studying Studio Arts to keep building my traditional foundation. I work mainly in Procreate, Photoshop, and Illustrator.

Every piece on this site is made by hand. I don't use AI in my work, because the process of drawing is the part I love most, and my clients deserve art that someone actually thought through line by line.

As a Designer Intern at WaBa Grill, I created a wide range of marketing pieces, mostly window clings, which taught me how to design for real spaces, real deadlines, and real brand guidelines. My work has also placed in the California Duck & Wildlife Stamp Art Contest, and I've done volunteer design work for DDIM Academy.

Outside of art, I've worked as an Assistant Manager, seasonal event staff, a cook, and a cashier. Those roles taught me how to work with people, stay calm under pressure, and get things done on time, and I bring that same reliability to every commission.
